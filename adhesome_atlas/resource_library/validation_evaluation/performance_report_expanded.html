A wrong positive-family call contributes one FP and one FN here. Admissible family labels come only from the trusted labels and ontology. This does not evaluate exact gene, isoform or paralogue names.

Broad-family policy alone accepts the explicitly declared actin, Src/Csk and PET–LIM ontology ancestors. These are ontology roll-ups rather than independently curated new gold labels. Specific-family policy retains distinct gold labels. Macro scores give equal weight to each admissible gold-family set, keeping alternative labels together, plus predicted-only wrong-family groups. Undefined group denominators are omitted from that mean and their counts are exported in family_macro_metrics.tsv; per-group decisions are in family_per_group_metrics.tsv.
