# Narrative calibration · 2026-10-10

The curator's narrative is reproduced verbatim in `curator_narrative_2026-10-10.txt`. `annotation_rules.json` records protein-specific and family-scoped orthogroup refinements. Explicit protein rules take precedence. `annotation_coverage.csv` is a snapshot of the current two audited catalogues: 210 of 616 candidates receive specific narrative annotations; every explicitly named protein occurs in the catalogue. General catalogue annotations remain for proteins not covered by the narrative.

Derived fields identify the candidate annotation, lineage, architecture, evolutionary context, matching basis and source. They appear in catalogue tables, dossiers, network node details and labels, orthology and phylogeny annotations, and hypothesis exports. `adhesome_interpretation` uses the narrative context for matched candidates; its previous value remains in `audit_adhesome_interpretation`. Original workbook classifications, family decisions, domains, motif evidence, quantitative scores, source orthogroups and reference interactions are preserved.

The refinements distinguish five architecture-retaining talin proteins, one partial-architecture talin-like candidate and three HIP1R-like proteins; conserved/parasite-restricted kindlin; canonical/catenin-related vinculin; alpha-actinin/spectrin; cofilin/twinfilin/drebrin/GMF; LIM-scaffold subfamilies; FAK domain differences; canonical ILK versus other kinases; Src/PH-BTK/ABL-related lineages; phosphatase subfamilies; collagen, laminin/netrin and FN3 receptor/giant-kinase architectures.

Vinculin clade orthogroup IDs come from the catalogue because the narrative does not assign its two listed IDs to individual clades. Unenumerated collagen and kinase lineages retain catalogue annotations. No extra interactions, numerical confidence scores, receptor-specific ligand binding or experimental validation are inferred from these refinements.

Orthology is contextual evolutionary evidence. These annotations use the narrative's domain architectures, topology and phylogenetic interpretation alongside the existing audit; the matching rules do not create an orthology-only adhesome-membership filter.
