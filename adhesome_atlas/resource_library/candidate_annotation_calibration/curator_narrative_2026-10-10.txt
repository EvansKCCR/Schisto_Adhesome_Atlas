Recovery of membrane-proximal integrin adaptors
Nine talin-related proteins were recovered across the three schistosome species. Five matched the configured talin architecture: MS3_00000278 in S. haematobium; EWB00_003146 and EWB00_006505 in S. japonicum; and Smp_167010 and Smp_343540 in S. mansoni. All five candidates were assigned to OG0001448 together with metazoan talin orthologues. MS3_00001770 also mapped to OG0001448 but showed only partial talin architecture. Phylogenetic analysis resolved the talin-like proteins into two conserved cross-species groups: EWB00_003146/Smp_343540/MS3_00001770 and EWB00_006505/Smp_167010/MS3_00000278. By contrast, EWB00_005902, Smp_246550, and MS3_00005288 shared only an I/LWEQ-containing partial architecture, mapped to OG0001835, and clustered with HIP1R-like proteins. 
Two representatives each of kindlin candidates were also identified S. haematobium (MS3_00003235 and MS3_00009375), S. japonicum (EWB00_002103 and EWB00_006747), and S. mansoni (Smp_053420 and Smp_070640). The candidates formed two conserved cross-species groups, which was distinguished by sequence length and orthology. The shorter proteins, MS3_00003235, EWB00_002103, and Smp_053420, were approximately 850 amino acids long, retained the configured kindlin-associated architecture, and belonged to a broadly conserved OG0001282. The longer proteins, MS3_00009375, EWB00_006747, and Smp_070640, were approximately 1,140-1,190 amino acids long and no direct pairwise orthologue detected (Schistosoma-restricted orthogroup, OG0013508). Both groups retained kindlin-associated FERM features. 
Conserved and lineage-expanded actin-linking proteins in Schistosoma
Vinculin-like proteins 
Six vinculin-family proteins were identified, with two representatives each in S. haematobium, S. japonicum and S. mansoni. Phylogeny separated these candidates into two well-supported clades.  MS3_00007047, EWB00_003565 and Smp_246050 grouped with canonical vinculin orthologues, whereas MS3_00007343, EWB00_001225 and Smp_130900 formed a second vinculin-family lineage associated with the broader catenin-related branch. All six proteins contained the characteristically extended vinculin-family architecture and falls into two separate orthogroups, OG0005046 and OG0001602 with broad metazoan orthology. 
α-actinin-like family 
The analysis recovered 15 α-actinin candidate proteins including five from S. haematobium, four from S. japonicum and six from S. mansoni. The α-actinin candidates were grouped into two broadly conserved orthogroups, OG0000625 and OG0000486 that share many-to-may orthology relationships with metazoan α-actinin family proteins as well as two parasites divergent orthogroups (OG0006689 and OG0000961). Within the OG0000486 broadly conserved orthogroups, candidates formed closely related triplets and group together broader spectrin-family radiation. The extended architecture of candidate proteins within OG0000486, including spectrin repeats and a C-terminal pleckstrin-homology domain, distinguishes it from the more compact canonical α-actinin orthogroup (OG0000625). The parasite divergent candidates orthogroup, OG0006689 (MS3_00010478 and Smp_315810) grouped with invertebrate spectrin-like sequences but lacked direct vertebrate orthologues. Smp_332820 represented a Schistosoma-restricted lineage, OG0000961. Smp_332820 retained 2 tandem calponin-homology, 5 spectrin-repeat and C-terminal pleckstrin-homology domain but have no direct orthologues from the 5 references we used for the current analysis. 
Cofilin/ADF-like family
Fifteen cofilin/ADF-family candidates were identified including five proteins from S. haematobium, four from S. japonicum and six from S. mansoni. Orthology and phylogeny resolved them into four distinct, strongly supported clades. The largest lineage, OG0000643 clustered within the broadly expanded metazoan cofilin/ADF group and consist of two candidate cofilin-like from each specie. By contrast, OG0002257 contained candidate proteins with twinfilin-like architecture, characterized by tandem ADF-homology domains (MS3_00005251, EWB00_007739, Smp_308420). The OG0002191 orthogroup represents a conserved triplet with drebrin-like ADF domains and C-terminal SH3 domains. The fourth lineage, OG0002291 represents candidates with GMF-like architecture including MS3_00006566 and Smp_328200. No S. japonicum candidate was recovered for the OG000229 orthogroup in the curated dataset.
Filamin-like family
The analysis recovered six filamin proteins that resolved into one orthogroup (OG0000767) that cluster together with metazoan filamin orthologues. OG0000767 orthogroup included two representative sequences each from S. haematobium, S. japonicum and S. mansoni with canonical filamin architecture: N-terminal tandem calponin-homology actin-binding domain followed by a repeated immunoglobulin-like filamin. Phylogenetic analysis separated the six candidates into two paralogous: MS3_00005799, EWB00_006234 and Smp_000100 form one clade whereas MS3_00007970, EWB00_002775 and Smp_317420 forms a closely related paralogue.  
Focal-adhesion scaffolds and signalling components
LIM domain-containing scaffolds
The recovered LIM-domain scaffold repertoire consists of distinct paxillin-, PINCH- and zyxin-related lineages. Overall, fifteen paxillin-like protein candidates were recovered and grouped into four conserved orthogroups (OG0002006, OG0000150, OG0000983, OG0000730) that maintained the basic repeated C-terminal LIM domains. OG0000983 orthogroup consist of sequences that has two C-terminal LIM domains (MS3_00003129, EWB00_009560, Smp_146200) and grouped together with mammalian LIM-related protein. OG0002006 orthogroup was characterised by 4 repeated C-terminal LIM domains, however, unlike the mammalian sequences, the Schistosoma orthologues (MS3_00001381, EWB00_003429, Smp_315700) lack the N-terminal paxillin-domain. The OG0000730 orthogroup included conserved Schistosoma sequences the contain an N-terminal PDZ-domain and 3 C-terminal LIM repeats (MS3_00006333, EWB00_008747 and Smp_157310) that groups with metazoan PDZ and LIM domain proteins. The broader orthogroup, OG0000150 included 2 sequences each from S. haematobium, S. japonicum and S. mansoni that resolved into two clades using phylogenetic analysis. The first clade included sequences that has N-terminal PET domain and 6 repeated C-terminal LIM domains (MS3_00002568, EWB00_003590, Smp_048560). This has previously been annotated in S. mansoni as SmLIMPETin. The second group included sequences with shorter length with 5 repeated C-terminal LIM domains and without the N-terminal PET domain (MS3_00003616, EWB00_009388, Smp_143130).
Two PINCH-like lineages were recovered which form two orthogroups. MS3_00001825, EWB00_009541 and Smp_165070 constitute a shared HOG (OG0002150) that is broadly conserved across metazoan lineages. The second orthogroup consist of a Schistosoma-only sampled HOG (MS3_00010441, EWB00_004122 and Smp_020540). Both lineages showed five-LIM architectures with PINCH-specific domain signatures. 
A separate zyxin-like clade was resolved that included shared HOG (OG0002012) with a 1:1:1 Schistosoma copy profile (MS3_00008284, EWB00_004965 and Smp_248010) and direct orthologues in multiple reference species. Members of OG0002012 are characterized by LIM2 Ajuba-like signatures. 
FAK- and ILK-like single-copy lineages
The FAK-family candidates recovered included MS3_00002824, EWB00_007322 and Smp_137610 that shared HOG with a 1:1:1 Schistosoma copy profile within a broadly conserved metazoan orthogroup (OG0002086). Only MS3_00002824 have the complete architecture of FAK-like architectures. EWB00_007322 lack the C-terminal Focal-AT domain, whereas Smp_137610 lacks the N-terminal FERM domain. The phylogenetic analysis, however, group them as one clade together with metazoan FAK-like proteins. 
The analysis recovered 3 ILK-like family candidates (MS3_00005632, EWB00_010398 and Smp_079760) that formed a shared HOG (OG0003976) with a 1:1:1 Schistosoma copy profile and one-to-one relationships with metazoan ILK. Other kinase-like orthogroups with shared HOG with a 1:1:1 Schistosoma copy profile with partial ILK-family configuration include OG0001365 (MS3_00008020, EWB00_001594, Smp_049760), OG0002154 (MS3_00007604, EWB00_007161, Smp_053560), OG0002218 (MS3_00002424, EWB00_008286, Smp_131800), OG0002878 (MS3_00006556, EWB00_007221, Smp_344780). Schistosoma members of OG0001365 include membrane protein with GS domain plus Serine/Threonine protein kinases, catalytic domain. OG0002154 include Schistosoma members that have MAPK-activated protein kinase 2-like architecture. OG0002218 resolved into serine/threonine-protein kinase Oxidative-stress-responsive kinase 1 (OSR1). 
Src-family kinases
Twenty-two Src-family proteins with fully configured family-domain architecture (SH3-SH2-kinase) were recovered which resolve into 5 orthogroups. The largest orthogroup (OG0000213) contains 3 sequences each for S. haematobium (MS3_00002771, MS3_00008738, MS3_00009264) and S. japonicum (EWB00_004406, EWB00_007520, EWB00_007873) but four for S. mansoni (Smp_006920, Smp_136300, Smp_172200, Smp_348670) that shares many-to-many relationship with broad metazoan Src kinases. OG0001055 represented shared HOG with a 1:1:1 Schistosoma copy profile and direct metazoan orthologues. Schistosoma candidates including MS3_00001484, EWB00_001471, Smp_124850 has the conserved SH3-SH2-kinase architecture in addition to N-terminal PH/BTK-domain. The third orthogroup (OG0001364) has a characteristics C-terminal F-actin binding domain in addition to the SH3-SH2-kinase signature and cluster together with Abelson tyrosine-protein kinase 2. OG0003193 (MS3_00004068, EWB00_003104, Smp_212730) and OG0002474 (MS3_00009653, EWB00_005849, Smp_125360) orthogroups have broad SH3-SH2-kinase architecture. 
Modular signalling adaptors
The analysis recovered conserved Nck-like and Shc-like candidates that shared HOGs with direct metazoan orthologues and exhibited a 1:1:1 Schistosoma copy profile. The recovered Nck candidates (MS3_00006664, EWB00_010643, and Smp_014850) retained the expected three-SH3-plus-SH2 architecture. The Shc candidates (MS3_00002046, EWB00_007978, and Smp_196040) also retained the diagnostic PTB-SH2 organization. The recovered Grb2 candidates comprised a broadly conserved lineage containing MS3_00002675, EWB00_003848, and Smp_062950, and a schistosome-restricted lineage represented by MS3_00003161 and Smp_044530. Candidates from both lineages retained the characteristic SH3-SH2-SH3 architecture. 
PTP-PEST-related phosphatases
The analysis recovered 10 PTP-PEST-related proteins that resolved into four orthogroups: OG0001988 (MS3_00003183, EWB00_008528, and Smp_139930), OG0002082 (MS3_00005371, EWB00_009224, and Smp_150220), OG0010632 (MS3_00009095, EWB00_002182, and Smp_156870), and OG0007341 (MS3_00011141). All candidates contained the characteristic C-terminal protein tyrosine phosphatase catalytic domain. Members of OG0001988 clustered with metazoan tyrosine-protein phosphatase non-receptor type 1. Schistosoma homologues in OG0002082 clustered with metazoan receptor-type tyrosine-protein phosphatase-like proteins but lacked the N-terminal RESP18 and receptor 1A2 domains. The OG0010632 orthogroup lacked direct mammalian orthologues but displayed a one-to-one relationship with Xenopus laevis.
Collagen-like, laminin-like and fibronectin type III-containing families
Collagen-like families
The analysis identified collagen-like repertoire that contains 6 independently evolving orthogroups. The most broadly conserved group, OG0000309, includes representatives from all three schistosome species and direct orthologues from the 5 reference proteomes. Candidate proteins in OG0000309 show characteristics collagen alpha(IV) chain architecture, including predicted extracellular localization, multiple discontinuous collagen repeats and C-terminal C4 domains. OG0000309 includes three S. haematobium candidates (MS3_00000941, MS3_00001861, MS3_00010191) and 4 candidates each for S. japonicum (EWB00_001102, EWB00_006199, EWB00_006200, EWB00_006201) and S. mansoni (Smp_170340, Smp_159600, Smp_213900, Smp_213910). 
A second conserved lineage, OG0000784, contains collagen-like proteins with Collagen alpha (V) chain architecture - N-terminal laminin G-like/Thrombospondin N-terminal -like domain, collagen repeats and fibrillar collagens C-terminal domain. The orthogroup has direct vertebrate support and has 2:2:2 schistosome copy profile. OG0000784 include two candidates each from S. haematobium (MS3_00001716 and MS3_00010057), S. japonicum (EWB00_000274 and EWB00_001311) and S. mansoni (Smp_123830 and Smp_196840). 
OG0000283 constitutes shared collagen lineage supported by human, mouse and X. laevis orthologues. It is expanded to three copies in each S. haematobium (MS3_00005611, MS3_00007363 and MS3_00007701), S. japonicum (EWB00_007395, EWB00_008239, EWB00_010448) and S. mansoni (Smp_135560, Smp_164590, Smp_343740). Smp_164590 has a COL1A1 architecture characterized by N-terminal von Willebrand factor type C domain, collagen repeats and fibrillar collagens C-terminal domain. EWB00_010448 has a truncated architecture with a single collagen domain and fibrillar collagens C-terminal domain. The remaining has collagen alpha-2 (I) chain architectures characterized by collagen repeats with fibrillar collagens C-terminal domain. 
In contrast, OG0012988 and OG0013520 represented Schistosoma-only orthogroups with 1:1:1 schistosome profile. MS3_00005610, EWB00_010450 and Smp_197370 are members OG0012988 lineage that retain the conserved collagen repeats and fibrillar collagens C-terminal domain.  OG0013520 included shorter fragment of schistosome collagen-like candidates (range: 325-425 residues) with a single collagen domain. 
Laminin-like families
The laminin-like repertoire is distributed among four conserved metazoan orthogroups with predicted extracellular localization. OG0001608 represents a conserved laminin subunit beta chain-related lineage with a 1:1:1 schistosome profile and broad metazoan support. Candidate proteins (MS3_00000487, EWB00_002741, Smp_148790) are characterised by Laminin N-terminal domain followed by 13 laminin EGF-like repeats. The topology of OG0001608 supports an ancestral metazoan laminin-like scaffold with vertebrate paralog expansion. A second orthogroup, OG0001908, represents laminin subunit gamma chain-related lineage which has a 1:1:1 schistosome copy profile and broad metazoan orthologue support. It has a characteristic laminin N-terminal domain followed by 11 laminin EGF-like repeats like their broad metazoan orthologues. Schistosomes candidates represented include MS3_00004459, EWB00_006126 and Smp_163810. A third orthogroup, OG0002087 represents a conserved laminin subunit alpha-like lineage with a characteristic laminin N-terminal domain followed by 4 laminin EGF-like repeats, Laminin B domain, repeated laminin EGF-like repeats and terminal Laminin G domains. The schistosome candidates, MS3_00008957, EWB00_001328 and Smp_343650 have a divergent C-terminal Laminin G repeat
OG0001017 represents a Netrin-like lineage with broad metazoan support. It includes two copies from each of schistosome species with a characteristic Laminin N-terminal domain, 3 laminin-associated EGF repeats and Netrin C-terminal (C345C) domain. Schistosome candidates in this orthogroup include MS3_00003962, MS3_00004070, EWB00_002313, EWB00_003107, Smp_146840 and Smp_151310. 
Fibronectin type III (FN3)-containing families
The identified FN3-containing proteins resolved into 25 orthogroups, including seven Schistosoma-only HOGs with 1:1:1 schistosome copy profiles (OG0007305, OG0007310, OG0010555, OG0012358, OG0013645, OG0013657, and OG0013755). Candidate sequences in OG0007305 (MS3_00007223, EWB00_003342, and Smp_173100) represented ROBO-like receptors with four to five tandem Ig repeats followed by two FN3 domains. OG0007310 (MS3_00010298, EWB00_007901, and Smp_153170) represented UNC-89/obscurin/SPEG-like giant serine/threonine kinase-like proteins characterized by Ig and FN3 repeats, two catalytic protein kinase domains, and long low-complexity/coiled-coil regions. EWB00_007901 and Smp_153170 had consensus predictions of cytoplasmic localization, whereas MS3_00010298 had contradictory topology predictions and remains unresolved. Candidate proteins in OG0010555 (MS3_00010050, EWB00_005238, and Smp_124210) had Eph receptor/EphA4-like receptor tyrosine kinase architecture, characterized by an EPH ligand-binding domain followed by an Eph receptor cysteine-rich domain, FN3 domain, transmembrane region, and intracellular kinase. MS3_00010050 and Smp_124210 had consensus-predicted signal peptides (SPs) and transmembrane regions (TMs), whereas EWB00_005238 had a predicted TM without a predicted SP. OG0012358, OG0013645, and OG0013657 represented orthologues with DCC/neogenin-family receptor architectures. Members of OG0013755 (MS3_00011127, EWB00_009592, and Smp_343680) had characteristic 3 tandem Ig-like repeats, seven FN3 repeats, single-pass receptor topology, and tandem intracellular PTP domains typical of type IIa receptor protein tyrosine phosphatases (LAR/PTPRD/PTPRS-like).
