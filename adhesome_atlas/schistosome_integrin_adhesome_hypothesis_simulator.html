Mechanism and reproducibility

Ligand anchoring initializes AII = 1. Talin recruitment forms AIT = ATI = ATT = 1. When local force exposes talin sites, the effective T–V weight is multiplied by α; recruitment forms ATV = AVT = AVV = 1. An assembled I–T–V site can bind actin, which adds force at the next timestep. Actin-connected vinculin clutches increase the overload threshold by the editable reinforcement factor. Basal dissociation or overload clears the local graph, actin and force together.

Distinct receptor sites avoid overlapping integrin ownership. Both panels use the same seeded site placement and random variates. Exports include W, source associations, all parameters, full 4D lattice snapshots, force/actin histories and projected outputs. These normalized parameters are hypotheses for testing, not fitted affinities or physical force measurements.
