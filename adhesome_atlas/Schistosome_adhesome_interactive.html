Current hypothesis and data scope

Working hypothesis: schistosomes possess a conserved but parasite-adapted integrin-associated network that may couple candidate extracellular and host-vascular inputs to integrin-proximal adhesion machinery, actin remodelling, FAK/Src signalling, and an experimentally informed S. mansoni Smβ-Int1–ILK–PINCH–Nck2–SmVKR1 receptor-coupling branch.

Critical interpretation
Node counts report family-assignment records, not proteins proven to assemble in one complex. Most edges are transferred from reference adhesomes. Exact αβ pairing, endogenous ligands, host-interface effects, and family-wide complex assembly remain unresolved.

The experimentally informed reproductive branch is species-specific evidence from S. mansoni; its transfer to the other two species is a comparative hypothesis.
